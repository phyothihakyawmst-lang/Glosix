The Science

ClimateFit Complex™ —
built for tropical skin days

Hot, humid, on-the-go — Myanmar weather asks a lot of skin. Every Glosix body wash layers three hydrators under the cleanse, so the shower refreshes instead of strips.

01

Hyaluronic Acid

Attracts and holds water in the skin's surface — hydration that survives the rinse and keeps building after you towel off.

02

Vitamin E

An antioxidant comfort blanket — softens, smooths and helps skin stay supple through sun-soaked days.

03

Aloe Extract

Classic tropical soother — calms and cools while it hydrates, the perfect partner for HA in humid climates.

✦ HA verified in all 9 Glosix formulas
The Glow Ritual

Three steps. One glow.

A body-care routine as easy as your shower — cleanse, polish, seal in the glow.

Cleanse with Skincare

Pick your body wash — brightening peach, firming pomegranate, cooling marine or detox charcoal — and let HA-rich lather cleanse without stripping.

Polish, 2–3× a Week

Massage in Gluta Coffé, Charcoal Detox or Pomé Arbutin as a scrub — or leave on as a mask for a deeper, spa-level treatment.

Glow, Rinse After Rinse

HA keeps working after the water stops. Skin stays quenched, soft and luminous — the magic of glow, every single day.
